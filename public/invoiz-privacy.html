Privacy Policy

Invoiz ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Invoiz on your iPhone.

💾

Section 1

Data Storage

We do not collect any data. All data you create in Invoiz - including invoices, products, and business information - is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or transmit your data anywhere.

📷

Section 2

Camera Access

Invoiz requests access to your device camera to scan product barcodes and to read the QR code on an invoice when you verify it. Camera data is processed in real time on your device and is never saved, uploaded, or shared.

🖼️

Section 3

Photo Library Access

If you choose to add a business logo, Invoiz requests read access to your photo library for that single selection. The selected image is stored locally on your device only.

🔗

Section 4

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. When you choose to share an invoice PDF using the system share sheet, you control the destination - Invoiz has no involvement in that transfer.

👶

Section 5

Children's Privacy

Invoiz is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

📝

Section 6

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be available within the app. Continued use of Invoiz after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

✉️

Section 7

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at fredycamasdev@gmail.com.
